Each level: 24 cans flash, one at a time. Tap every white can ⚡ for +10 (+streak bonus). Hold still on black cans ⚫ — tapping one costs a ❤️. Missing a white can costs a ❤️ too. Higher levels = faster cans, more black cans. 3 misses/fumbles and the run ends.
